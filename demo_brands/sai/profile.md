# Sai Kanagat: one-page profile

Everything here is stated on https://saikanagat.com (read 2026-11-16). Claim IDs point to claims.json. Items marked "conflicting" in claims.json are left out or flagged here. Not a pitch, a fact sheet.

## Who he is

An industrial designer and digital marketer based in Bologna, Italy (C01). He studied industrial design in India (C05), moved into digital marketing, and is finishing an MSc in Digital Marketing & Communication at Bologna Business School (C03). He speaks English fluently and Hindi and Tamil natively (C18, the Italian level is disputed, see G03). His own framing: the rare one who is both designer and marketer.

Tagline: "Design things that sell." Two operating modes: "Made to last" (the design side) and "Built to grow" (the marketing side) (C29).

## What he offers

Two things, both public:

1. A full-time role in the EU across marketing, brand strategy, creative direction, marketing engineering or product, with a stated preference for teams that do not split design and growth (C19).
2. Project work across brand identity, paid media (Meta and Google Ads), social and content, SEO, email and CRM, visual merchandising and product design (listed on the index and about pages).

No priced package is published (see GAPS.md).
